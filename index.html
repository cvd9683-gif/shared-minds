Shared Minds

Weekly assignments · Cathy Doss · ITP

  1. Week 1

    Thought Forest

    Type a thought and it takes root. Each one becomes a small procedural tree that grows into a swaying forest, so a stream of consciousness ends up as something you can stand in and look at. An Overwhelm slider drives how hard the whole forest moves.

    Open the forest Source on GitHub

  2. Week 2

    Learning the Steps, Feeling the Dance

    Take turns with a dancer. You teach it a phrase of four to eight moves using the arrow keys and the space bar, in whatever rhythm you like; it answers with a phrase of its own. A language model makes that answer from nothing but your move names and the millisecond gaps between them — it never sees the figure, or you — and both phrases are drawn at the same scale so one can be read against the other.

    Teach the dancer Source on GitHub The dancer really answers — a language model reads your rhythm and dances back. A few turns each, since it runs on a class budget.

  3. Week 3

    Do You See What I Mean?

    Pictionary, except you paint with your face. You get a simple prompt and 90 seconds; your webcam watches your head and face, and those movements steer the brush. Afterwards your face and your painting replay side by side — the machine measured every expression, but did it ever understand what you were trying to draw?

    Paint with your face Source on GitHub

  4. Week 4

    Music Map

    A song has a history before it becomes part of yours. Move through tracks by the date they were saved, then open one into a map of the recordings, samples, interpolations and people connected to it. Each route is recorded as a journey, saved under your name, so you can return to the original track, listen again, and note what you hear now.

    Open the map Source on GitHub Runs on a fictional demo collection until you connect Spotify.

  5. Week 5

    My Dancing Trees

    A habit tracker where every habit you keep grows a singing plant on your own island. The plants stand together like a choir and sing one song, and friends sail between islands to leave each other plants. Finish a habit for a week and you grow something rarer, which can grow rarer still when a friend keeps it.

    Grow your island Source on GitHub The friends are example islands for now. Press play for sound.
