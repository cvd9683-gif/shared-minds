Eyes Closed

Thoughts placed by what they mean, not when or where they happened.

Close your eyes for ten seconds.

Then write down whatever was there. Not what you saw. What was there.

Each thought finds its place by what it means, beside other thoughts, yours and other people's, that mean something close. Nothing here is arranged by time or by where you were.

A small language model runs inside this page. What you write stays in your browser. Sound is part of it: close thoughts sound close.
